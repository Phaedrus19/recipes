Smoked Burnt End Sweet BBQ Wings

Home

Description

Smoked wings that are easy, quick, and tasty!

Ingredients

  • 4 lbs raw Chicken Wings
  • BBQ Rub of Your Choice
  • 12 oz BBQ Sauce
  • Honey
  • Suggested wood chips: Apple

Instructions

  1. Season raw chicken wings generaously with your favorite BBQ rub
  2. Place approximately 2 pounds per smoker rack (15-20 wings) and smoke for 45 minutes at 275°F
  3. Remove chicken wings from smoker. Toss in your favorite BBQ sauce
  4. Place wings on top rack of smoker with a disposable tray (or drip pan) below. Drizzle honey evenly over wings
  5. Cook for an additional 15-20 minutes until crisp and the internal temperature is 175-180°F
  6. Remove wings and serve hot
