Smoked St. Louis Ribs

Home

Description

Tender and delicious ribs!

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp BBQ Rub
  • 3 tbsp BBQ Sauce
  • 4 tbsp Butter
  • 4 ozs Juice
  • 2 tbsp Sriracha Sauce
  • 3 lbs St. Lous Style Pork Spare Ribs

Instructions

  1. Rub ribs with Sriracha sauce and seaon liberally with your favorite BBQ rub
  2. Set up grill to smoke at 250°F
  3. Smoke ribs at 250 degrees uncovered for the first 3 hours
  4. Wrap the ribs in foil with butter and juice and smoke the ribs wrapped for 2 hours
  5. Unwrap the ribs. Make a foil boat and finish smoking the ribs unwrapped for 30 minutes to 1 hour
  6. Sauce up the ribs if you like, slice, and enjoy!
