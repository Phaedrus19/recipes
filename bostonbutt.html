Smoked Boston Butt

Home

Description

Boston Butt is a delicious cut of beef from the shoulder area of the cow. This simple recipes is time consuming at ~10 hours, but delicious all the same.

Ingredients

  • Pork Shoulder ~8 lbs
  • Dry Rub of Your Choice
  • Yellow Mustard

Instructions

  1. Season pork butt generously with dry rub seasoning
  2. Place pork in a resealable plastic bag. Spread mustard evenly over butt while inside the bag to avoid mess
  3. Seal bag and refrigerate over night
  4. Preheat smoker to 225°F
  5. Place butt on smoker and smoke for 7-8 hours or until internal temperature reaches 185°F
  6. Cover with heavy duty aluminum foil and return to smoker. Increase temperature to 275°F. Smoke for an additional 2 hours or until internal temperature reaches 200°F
  7. Let meat rest inside foil for 30 minutes
  8. Shred meat and serve
